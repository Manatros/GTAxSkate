Tutorial Island optional package
--------------------------------

The tutorial archive contains GTAxSkate and SK8V preparation/installation
tools, not third-party game assets. Their notices are provided as LICENSE.txt,
NOTICE.txt, LICENSE-SK8V.txt and NOTICE-SK8V.txt inside that archive.

No GTA V, Skate 3 or other retail game assets are included in either optional
download. Users must provide game files they are entitled to use.
